## Interpretation

This is one host-level FIO snapshot of sequential 128 KiB reads at queue depth 1 for about 10 seconds against a 256 MiB file that was reused (`time_based=1`). It does not identify the storage device, does not bound peak SSD performance, and does not describe NAND, FTL, or garbage collection. Full caveats: [experiments/EXP001.md](../experiments/EXP001.md).
